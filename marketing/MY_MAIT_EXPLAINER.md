# My Mait

*Your companion. Yours to shape. Yours to keep.*

Authored 2026-05-30. v1 product explainer. Repurposable for landing copy, onboarding, or a deck. Source of truth: WIKI "Applet Gate Manifest + My Mait Product Model".

---

## The one-liner

My Mait is a free AI companion that lives on your machine, comes with a personality from the first second, and becomes more yours every time you use it. Not a chatbot you rent. A character you own.

## The insight

Everyone else is selling you the same thing: a smarter model behind a login. ChatGPT, Claude, Character.AI, they compete on whose brain is bigger, and they all own the relationship, not you.

My Mait competes somewhere they aren't playing: **ownership and identity.** Your Mait is not a window into someone's server. It runs locally, it has a face you made, and it carries traits and skills you actually own. The question stops being "which AI is smartest" and becomes "what is *my* Mait like." That is a different game, and it is stickier.

You don't rent a Mait. You raise one.

## The experience

Three beats, and only three:

1. **Download My Mait.** It is free. No tier, no trial, no "Lite."
2. **Meet your Mait.** It arrives with a starter personality, cheerful, competent, helpful. You are not staring at a blank prompt box wondering what to type. You meet someone.
3. **Shape your Mait.** Everything after that is discovery. Give it new traits. Teach it new skills. Give it a different face. The Mait you have in a month is one you built.

"Download. Meet. Shape." That is an ecosystem. "Download Lite, upgrade to Full" is a SaaS funnel, and we are deliberately not building that.

## What it does for you

Your Mait is the front door to everything in Everywear. Ask it to make a song, build a video, generate an image, organise your work, and it quietly handles the machinery behind the scenes. You never see the engine names or the plumbing. You talk to your Mait; your Mait gets it done.

## Free, and honest about it

The base Mait is genuinely free and genuinely useful, not a crippled demo. We make money the way a world makes money, not the way a subscription does:

- **The Trading Post.** An in-app store of **trait shards** and **skill shards**, the personality and the abilities you bolt onto your Mait. Buy what you want, own what you buy, often for less than a dollar.
- That is it. No paywall on the companion itself. The companion is the point; the shards are the economy.

## As smart as your rig

Your Mait runs on your hardware, privately. The better your machine, the more capable the model it runs, decided automatically when you install. A modest laptop gets a lean, fast Mait; a serious GPU gets a serious one. You are never charged more for a smarter Mait. Your rig is the upgrade path, and your data never leaves the building.

## Give it a face

Avatar Studio lets you build and customise your Mait's look, free to start, with premium avatar pieces in the Trading Post. A companion you can see is a companion you keep.
